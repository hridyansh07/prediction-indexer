# Milestone-conditioned leader/follower

Runs an offline, long-only hypothetical target-claim position scenario after a causal leader-book innovation. It compares target-only, target plus released milestones, and target plus milestones plus leader models on the same observer decision schedule. Each frozen model has independent cash, positions and conservative capacity. Their P&L cannot be added. Forecasts and detection episodes never book cash or closed profit.

Factory: `replay.strategies.milestone_leader_follower:build`. Readers: `read_provisional` for content and `read_completed` for a supervisor SUCCESS run. Bench check: `replay.strategies.milestone_leader_follower:check`. The existing generic entry-point loader, strategy adapter, supervisor and bench call these functions directly. See [config.example.json](config.example.json), [bench.example.json](bench.example.json) and the governing [SPEC](SPEC.md).

Replace snapshot, fee, game and revision pins and supply reviewed native assets, fee bindings and rule bindings before use. Empty bindings admit no economic routes. Each rule binding has exactly `instrument`, `orientation`, `rule_sha256`, `alignment_id`, `price_increment_atoms`, and `quantity_increment_atoms`. Alignment IDs attest reviewed normal-resolution equivalence; masks alone do not establish payout rules. Decimal inputs are exact canonical strings; outputs use reduced rational strings. `source_revision` accepts a lowercase 40-character Git commit identity or a 64-character reviewed source-content SHA-256. It is an experiment pin, not an output hash.

Primary runs require an available pinned prepared game file. `book_only_comparison` is explicitly separate and ignores game constraints. Dynamic relations use exhaustive captured series masks and every released segment result. Unknown winners preserve uncertainty; independently indexed later winners remain usable. Score quality and ordered winner-prefix quality are separate. New identity/complement transformations warm up a full window. Implication, reverse implication, exclusion, overlap and unsupported shapes stay visibly unavailable; no identity response is guessed. Gameplay details are never inspected.

Models declare `ASSUMED_RESPONSE_MODEL` or `PAST_EVENT_FIT`. Assumed coefficients and fallback cohorts are explicit assumptions. Past-event models pin the same whole-event training/validation split, purged outcome horizons, cutoff, feature/target definitions and hyperparameter/eligibility identities. Cutoffs precede the run and exclude its event. This package consumes frozen reviewed coefficients; it does not train or claim empirical leadership from one event. Unknown-score fallback must explicitly cover unknown score. The template's unit response and zero baselines are illustrative, not calibrated.

The runtime stages same-time books, scopes and facts before one decision. Bounded as-of histories retain a predecessor and reset on every invalidation, including invalidate/restore at the same timestamp. Quiet timers cover window endpoints, model elapsed buckets, delay, rearm, entry-anchored horizon, settlement and holding-cost stops. Pending attempts freeze leader/target/model/proof/innovation and maximum size; quantity changes may shrink a delayed entry. A changed price/proof or missing qualifying history cancels it. Re-entry requires continuous positive-duration false signal, a newer price revision, cooldown and no residual holding.

Every native BUY and SELL uses the existing FeeBridge and an exact best-first native walk. Forecast bids shift eligible depth, clip probability, round down to native price increments and merge equal prices. Economics value retained post-fee holdings, future SELL fees, analytical charges and the initial loss gate. Opening alone debits cash and physical ask-source capacity. Cash, budgets, outstanding cost and concurrency are checked per account. `CUMULATIVE_DISPLAY_CAP` preserves price and total-side debits across repricing/restoration/scopes. `ISOLATED_NONADDITIVE` is detection/counterfactual-only and cannot publish repeated-depth additive positions.

Settlement, later match/segment releases, horizon, stop and take-profit have the specified tie priority. Latched exits sell eligible native increments and retain partial/dust holdings; retries require relevant target bid/validity/fee changes. Normal payout uses the first supported release plus the declared availability delay. Pinned payout uses evidenced availability; neither credits before acquisition. Contradictions suspend assumed resolution. Scope departure retains original owned identity/basis and leaves its unobservable book unpriced. Run-end residuals are censored; full-position P&L exists only after complete sale/payout. Holding charges remain analytical and never alter the cash ledger.

Outputs use a closed version-2 format; config/policy versions remain 1 and existing strategy schemas/goldens remain unchanged. This reader rejects superseded V1 scenario transport. Version 2 uses exact sparse book deltas, SHA-256-bound templates, fixed short field symbols and scalar size valuations which the reader independently reprices from committed native books.

- `decisions.ndjson`: staged native book attestations, reset evidence, released knowledge, admission and all three model observations.
- `actions.ndjson`: immutable attempts, delays/cancellations/skips, actual hypothetical orders, proportional FIFO basis, cash and holdings.
- `episodes.ndjson` and `denominators.ndjson`: maximal positive intervals and measurable/unavailable time per target/model.
- `predictions.ndjson`: common-schedule forecasts, observed/censored horizon outcomes, errors and explicitly nonadditive counterfactual nets.
- `positions.ndjson`: every acquired lot, partial results, residual basis, current bid liquidation marks and P&L only after complete closure.
- `objects.ndjson`: dense, SHA-256-bound relationship and observation templates, bounded to 33 MiB. Dynamic history endpoints and native book changes stay explicit. Prediction records batch at most 768 forecasts at one observer time; each file retains its 512 MiB, 1,000,000 physical-record and 4 MiB line limits.
- `summary.json`, `manifest.json`, `content_receipt.json`: native cash, capacity, costs, skips, residuals, per-event comparisons, pins and identities. Single-event uncertainty is unavailable and carries no corpus inference.

The independent reader streams bounded records through a disposable SQLite index with indexed as-of joins. It rebuilds causal histories/releases, relation/model arithmetic, native fee assessments, depleted entry repricing, order clocks/rules, signal/re-entry lifecycle, cash/holdings/capacity, settlement availability, censored marks, maximal episodes and totals. `obligations.py` derives required signals, pending dispositions, exits, residual retries and settlement at every committed time. Deleting an entire position ledger or relabelling a required exit as censored cannot pass. Book records attest the supplied replay reconstruction; reading does not replay raw payloads again. Completed reading also requires supervisor SUCCESS and binds its factory/config/content receipt. Content/manifests are fsynced before publishing the receipt commit marker.

Contradictions are derived from released facts and supported pinned exhaustive series spaces before admission. The reader reconstructs the complete candidate union, all size availability reasons and gates, deterministic leader selection, alternates and route counts. Pending selection retains its original leader/proof. Entry revalidation must use the audited current histories and route. A claimed unknown fee, thin book or missing history cannot suppress an executable entry, exit or forecast counterfactual. Independently reconstructed current books, native valuations and scalar size results each use a separate 128-entry reader cache; depleted entry repricing bypasses valuation caches. Candidate selection evidence stays in temporary SQLite as scalar metadata, whose disk usage is reported by the opt-in benchmark.

The supervised worker publishes its summary from bounded counters and native account state. The completion reader independently recomputes and compares it after supervisor completion, avoiding a duplicate long audit before terminal acknowledgement. Observation valuations use bounded scalar caches under unchanged native books/relation and the FeeBridge's pinned reference-time catalog. The reader has its own independently populated cache. Actual ledger orders and delayed/depleted entry repricing always receive fresh fee assessments and exact observer clocks.

Review corrections: book-only comparison consumes no game facts, game-derived quiet timers, contradiction constraints or normal game resolution; separately pinned settlement remains available. Overlapping cohorts reject at config time. Unsupported implications/overlaps report `MODEL_UNAVAILABLE` even before history warm-up. Per-book history overflow starts a visible `HISTORY_BOUND_EXCEEDED` interval and full-window warm-up instead of terminating the event. Size selection maximizes forecast net among sizes passing all gates. Price forecast error remains measurable on thin books or when no entry size is executable, while counterfactual P&L stays unavailable. Held claims freeze their original exhaustive outcome semantics for later normal settlement; the existing SDK also preserves document spaces across ordinary scope departure.

`replay/tests/test_leader_follower_repairs.py` contains the review regressions. An opt-in synthetic volume check drives real preparation and Decoder with 100-level ladders and all model roles: `python -m replay.tests.benchmark_leader_follower --changes 100000`. It reports every output file's size/count, playback, worker finish, maximum callback and independent audit separately; no live evidence or services are touched.

The full 100,000-change check at revision `b923b1b` produced 702,115,222 bytes across seven files: decisions 277,892,873 bytes and predictions 424,195,893 bytes, both within the 512 MiB per-file limit. Playback took 307.73 seconds, terminal finish 0.020 seconds and its independent audit 1540.03 seconds. Later reader completeness repairs receive a separate proportional benchmark; the older full audit does not verify those later repairs. These synthetic quantity changes establish volume handling, not execution latency or calibrated predictive value.

With the stronger reader, the proportional 2,000-change check took 6.42 seconds of playback, 0.028 seconds of terminal finish and 35.12 seconds of independent audit. The maximum callback was 0.019 seconds. Persisted outputs totaled 14,541,930 bytes; temporary SQLite used 189,104,128 bytes. A linear extrapolation suggests roughly 9.45 GB of transient audit disk for 100,000 similar changes, separate from persisted output limits; that disk projection and final-reader 100,000-change timing are unmeasured. Candidate rows retain only independent selection scalars, while the index also retains causal books, audited observations and decisions for cross-file completeness checks.

Offline contracts in `replay/tests/test_milestone_leader_follower.py` exercise real preparation, Decoder, readers and bench checks using hand-authored native shapes. No live venues, execution, object stores or deployments are added. The tests do not establish historical venue availability, trained predictive value or live Redis/Docker acceptance. Stateful support stays within this strategy and reuses SDK/fee primitives; the stateless SDK and existing artifacts remain compatible.
